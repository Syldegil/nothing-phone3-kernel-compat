# Limitations

Excluding KSU compilation units retains DWARF but removes KSU types/functions from BTF introspection. It does not inject stock BTF. Matching OEM bytes is a narrow compatibility gate, not a security or runtime proof. Bisimulation is not graph isomorphism or one-to-one relocation. CRC agreement alone is not semantic ABI compatibility. Parsers are research tools, not hardened adversarial-file validators.

TMPFS_XATTR changes are a distinct incompatible variant; no safe persistent verdict is claimed. Historical RAM/persistent results are bounded and firmware-specific. Calls, audio, tactile functions, real cameras, invasive SUSFS features, recovery and endurance were not comprehensively tested. Spoofed properties do not establish a locked bootloader. No global security claim or Nothing OS5 transfer is made.
