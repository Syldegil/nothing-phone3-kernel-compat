# Contributing

Provide a minimal synthetic reproduction and tool versions. Redact credentials, device IDs, account names, private domains, host paths and infrastructure. Do not attach firmware, personal logs, packet captures, conversations or signatures. Test your change in isolation; never use production systems as public CI runners.

ChatGPT/Codex assisted extraction, adaptation, documentation and bounded validation. Tests and independent automated reviews are limited evidence; they do not replace maintainer and downstream review. No private conversations or histories are included.
